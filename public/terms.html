Terms of Use

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Educational use only. SurgiQuiz supports learning, revision, and formative assessment. It is not a substitute for clinical supervision, institutional protocols, credentialing, or professional medical judgment.

Acceptable use

You may use SurgiQuiz for personal and professional surgical education. Do not use the service to upload unlawful content, confidential patient information, or material you do not have permission to process.

Medical content

Questions, explanations, AI outputs, anatomy assets, and learning recommendations may contain errors or become outdated. Clinically important information should be checked against current authoritative guidance and local practice.

Exam profiles

MRCS-oriented, ABSITE-oriented, Arab Board-oriented, Global Core, and other profiles are learning orientations unless explicitly stated otherwise. They do not imply endorsement, affiliation, or official blueprint certification by any examination body.

AI features

AI-generated content is probabilistic and must be reviewed. Do not rely on AI output for diagnosis, treatment, operative decisions, or patient-specific recommendations.

Availability

Features, models, question sets, and regional pathways may change as the product evolves. Some languages may use English fallback for reviewed medical content while specialist translation is incomplete.

Professional responsibility

Users remain responsible for their own professional conduct, patient confidentiality obligations, and compliance with applicable institutional and legal requirements.
