Privacy Notice

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Core principle: SurgiQuiz is an educational product. Do not upload identifiable patient information, confidential hospital records, or protected health information.

Information stored on your device

The current product stores learning preferences, language, exam orientation, quiz attempts, and knowledge-profile signals in browser storage so the experience can adapt to you.

AI and document features

When server-side AI features are enabled, text that you submit to AI Viva, AI Coach, case generation, or PDF-to-Quiz may be processed by configured inference providers. Use only educational, synthetic, public, or properly de-identified material.

Patient data

SurgiQuiz is not intended to receive patient identifiers. The SurgiCore-to-SurgiQuiz learning workflow must use de-identified educational summaries only. Users remain responsible for institutional, professional, and legal requirements that apply to clinical information.

Third-party infrastructure

The web application may use hosting, source-control, and AI inference infrastructure. Provider-specific processing is subject to the configuration and agreements used by the operator of the deployment.

Your control

You can reset locally stored learning progress from within the application. Browser storage can also be cleared using your browser settings.

International use

Privacy, medical-data, and educational regulations differ by jurisdiction. Availability of the application does not by itself establish regulatory compliance for clinical data processing in any country.
