Medical Safety

Last updated: 19 August 2026

SurgiQuiz is a learning system, not a clinical decision-support system. Patient care decisions must follow qualified clinical judgment, current evidence, and local protocols.

AI Viva and AI Coach

AI responses are formative educational feedback. They are not certification, credentialing decisions, or authoritative grading. Scoring should be interpreted as a learning signal only.

Case-based learning

Branching cases simplify real clinical complexity for education. They should not be used as protocol engines for actual patients.

Surgical anatomy

Anatomy diagrams and generated or reconstructed 3D assets require expert validation before they are treated as authoritative operative references. Variants, distortion, pathology, and operative perspective can change anatomy substantially.

PDF-to-Quiz

Generated questions should remain draft educational items until the source, explanation, and answer are checked. Source grounding reduces unsupported claims but does not eliminate model or extraction error.

Exposure versus competence

Case counts, operative roles, learning activity, and quiz performance may guide learning priorities. They must not be interpreted as proof of competence, readiness for independent practice, privileging, or credentialing.

High-risk information

For emergency management, medication doses, operative steps, complications, and guideline-sensitive decisions, verify information against current authoritative references and institutional protocols.
